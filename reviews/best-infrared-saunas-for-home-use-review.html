Buying Guide: What to Consider When Choosing an Infrared Sauna

Types of Infrared Heat

Understanding the three types of infrared heat is crucial for making the right choice:

  • Near-Infrared: Penetrates skin surface, promotes wound healing and skin rejuvenation
  • Mid-Infrared: Penetrates deeper, improves circulation and pain relief
  • Far-Infrared: Deepest penetration, promotes detoxification and weight loss

Full-spectrum saunas offer all three types, while most budget models focus on far-infrared only.

Size and Space Requirements

Consider both your available space and intended usage:

  • 1-Person: 36-40" wide, ideal for individual use
  • 2-Person: 45-60" wide, comfortable for couples or solo users who want extra room
  • 3-4 Person: 60"+ wide, great for families but requires significant space

Don't forget to account for ventilation space around the unit (typically 6" on all sides).

Construction Materials

Cedar: Traditional choice with natural antimicrobial properties and pleasant aroma, but requires more maintenance.

Hemlock: Lighter color, minimal maintenance, hypoallergenic, but lacks cedar's natural scent.

Basswood: Completely hypoallergenic, preferred for those with sensitivities, neutral appearance.

Electrical Requirements

Most home infrared saunas operate on standard 120V outlets, drawing 15-20 amps. However, larger units or those with advanced features may require 220V connections. Always verify your electrical capacity before purchasing.

EMF Levels

Electromagnetic field (EMF) emissions are a concern for many users. Look for saunas with EMF levels below 3mG (milligauss) at seating distance. Higher-quality models typically have better EMF shielding.

Warranty and Support

A comprehensive warranty indicates manufacturer confidence in their product:

  • Heaters: Should have at least 5-year warranty, preferably lifetime
  • Electrical components: 3-5 years minimum
  • Wood: 3-5 years for structural issues
  • Overall: Look for companies offering strong customer support and service networks

Assembly Requirements

Most infrared saunas require self-assembly. Consider your DIY skills and available help:

  • Budget 2-4 hours for assembly
  • Most require 2 people
